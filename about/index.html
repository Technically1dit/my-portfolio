Professional Practice

From data pipelines to conversational agents.

As a Machine Learning Engineer at Rootle in Ahmedabad, I architect AI-driven solutions designed for scale. My daily focus revolves around conversational AI agents, high-performance Python pipelines, Generative AI models, and the engineering required to transition theoretical models into reliable production utilities.

That foundational experience instilled a system-first discipline: writing clean code, designing robust abstractions, and never treating machine learning as an uninspected black box.

Beyond the code, I am an enthusiastic, curious, and collaborative teammate. I bring optimism, humor, and high intellectual dedication to every team and project I contribute to.
